Why an Ambulance Ride Can Cost More Than a Plane Ticket

(alt titles for A/B test: "Your Ambulance Bill Is Mostly Waiting Time" / "The $1,100 Ambulance Ride, Decoded")

=== DESCRIPTION ===
The average U.S. domestic plane ticket costs $445. The average ground ambulance bill for a privately insured patient was about $1,093 back in 2021, often for a ride of a few miles that you didn't choose. We decode where that money goes: out-of-network ambulances, the hidden cost of being ready 24/7, three different prices for the same ride, and the hole in the No Surprises Act. Plus what to do if a big ambulance bill lands in your mailbox.
